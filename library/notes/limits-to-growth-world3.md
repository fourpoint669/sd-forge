# World3 / Limits to Growth, pointers [unverified]
- Dynamics of Growth in a Finite World (1974) documents the equations of World3 sector by sector (population, capital, agriculture, nonrenewable resources, pollution).
- Limits to Growth (1972) and the 30-year update (2004) discuss scenarios (standard run, doubled resources, etc.), the behavior mode being overshoot and collapse.
- Open implementations, not vendored here (MANIFEST pointer rows `pointer:pyworld3` and `pointer:worlddynamics-jl`): pyworld3 (Python port, CeCILL-2.1), WorldDynamics.jl (Julia, MIT). The port's numerics differ slightly from the original; compare scenarios qualitatively, not to the last digit.
Source pointers: `pointer:meadows-limits-to-growth-1972`, `pointer:meadows-dynamics-of-growth-1974`.
